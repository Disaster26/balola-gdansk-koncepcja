What is the occasion?

  • A date

    A couples’ massage in the duo room, followed by dinner at the hotel restaurant. We will help you combine the two.

  • Birthdays

    Treatments for you and your loved ones, with refreshments arranged in advance.

  • Hen parties

    Time for the bride-to-be and her friends: treatments, refreshments and a calm spa atmosphere. Details agreed in advance.

  • A treatment together

    With your partner, a friend or your mum. Two people can enjoy treatments at the same time.

The duo room and HeadSPA Duo

In the duo room, two people enjoy a massage at the same time, side by side. HeadSPA Duo is a scalp and hair ritual that two people can also enjoy together.

On request we will prepare refreshments at the spa or help you combine your visit with dinner at the hotel restaurant. You can also add the private sauna area.

We do not perform HeadSPA on hair extensions, very long or very curly hair. We also advise against it straight after colouring: the trichological products may make fresh colour fade faster.
