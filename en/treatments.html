Body rituals and HeadSPA

The Amber Ritual wraps you in the warm scent of amber, while Yuzu refreshes with Japanese citrus. Both begin with a full-body scrub, followed by a mask and massage. HeadSPA is a ritual for scalp and hair: scrub, wash, mask, head and neck massage, and in the advanced version also a steam sauna, aromatherapy and a scalp infusion. We do not perform it on hair extensions, very long or very curly hair. We advise against HeadSPA straight after colouring, as the trichological products may make fresh colour fade faster.
