Brands we work with

We chose our skincare products above all for their proven, exceptional effectiveness, achieved through the innovative formulas they use. The cosmeceuticals available at our spa are among the very best cosmetological products in the world.

RVB LAB

RVB LAB is an Italian professional skincare brand created for advanced, effective and safe skin care. Its formulas combine innovative technologies with the highest-quality active ingredients to restore the skin’s healthy look, radiance and comfort.

RVB LAB products draw on cosmetologists’ many years of experience, so they meet the needs of different skin types - from dry and dehydrated to sensitive skin or skin in need of intensive recovery. The range includes lines for hydration, nourishment, anti-ageing and protection against harmful environmental factors.

All RVB LAB products are developed and made in Italy, which guarantees the highest quality and attention to every detail. It is skincare trusted by professionals in beauty salons around the world.

PROXN

PROXN is a Polish professional skincare brand created for effective and safe skin care. Its formulas combine advanced scientific knowledge with the highest-quality active ingredients, giving visible results from the very first uses. PROXN products support the skin’s recovery, renewal and protection against external factors, restoring a healthy look, firmness and radiance.

The range includes lines for different needs - from intensive hydration and anti-ageing therapies to care for sensitive and problem skin. PROXN works both in professional treatment rooms and in a home skincare routine. Thanks to precisely chosen ingredients and high concentrations of active substances, skincare becomes not only effective but also safe and enjoyable.

PROXN - Polish quality and professional skincare you can trust.

Diego dalla Palma Professional

Diego dalla Palma Professional is an Italian premium skincare brand combining cosmetologists’ experience with a passion for skincare. Products with rich formulas and high concentrations of active ingredients deliver effective hydration, recovery and protection, restoring a youthful look and radiance. The range includes face and body care lines for both professional and home use. The brand also offers the gentle Sensitive line, created for sensitive and demanding skin, including people after oncological therapy. Diego dalla Palma is advanced skincare tailored to individual needs and valued by professionals worldwide.

IMAGE Skincare

IMAGE Skincare was built on scientific advances in dermatology, aesthetic medicine and cosmetology. It is an American medical brand created by passionate people.

A few facts about IMAGE Skincare

IMAGE Skincare was founded by Janna Ronert and her husband, Dr Marc Ronert. Their aim was to create powerful products that reach the deeper layers of the epidermis. This makes it a complete programme: effective treatments are completed by home care. The treatments can be done safely all year round, despite the high concentrations of chemical peels. The brand is trusted by doctors, aesthetic medicine specialists and owners of clinics and spas. The most important argument is that the treatments give fast and long-lasting results in slowing down ageing and fighting its first signs. A further advantage is that the products contain no parabens, silicones, artificial colours or fragrances, and are based only on selected treasures of nature, mainly plant-derived ingredients.

Intraceuticals

Intraceuticals is an Australian cosmetology brand founded in 2002 by Anthony McMahon. Its philosophy combines scientific achievements, modern technology and professional skincare. The brand became famous worldwide for its innovative oxygen infusion method, which uses pressurised oxygen and advanced formulas rich in hyaluronic acid, vitamins, antioxidants and peptides, among others.

What makes the brand unique is the combination of professional in-clinic treatments with well-matched home care. The signature Intraceuticals technology uses pressurised oxygen to help apply specialist serums, delivering active ingredients and intense hydration. Its own layered hyaluronic acid system helps restore firmness, elasticity and smoothness and improves skin tone and the look of fine lines.

The range includes therapies for different skin needs - from intensive hydration and rejuvenation to brightening and evening out skin tone, and care for problem skin. Intraceuticals treatments give visible results after the first session, and when done regularly together with home care they help maintain and strengthen the results.

Intraceuticals has earned recognition from beauty specialists and professional spas around the world. It is a choice for people who expect modern, non-invasive care that combines the pleasure of a relaxing treatment with advanced technology and attention to skin condition.

Arosha

Arosha products are made exclusively for body treatments. AROSHA treatments are recommended for cellulite, reducing localised fat, contouring and slimming the figure, and firming and hydrating the skin.

The flagship AROSHA products are bandages made of soft cotton soaked in active ingredients. The idea of wrapping the body came from the need to contour the figure. Wrapped in a cocoon of active ingredients, the body absorbs them much better, because it happens gradually. No heat builds up under the bandage, so it does not dehydrate the tissue. The bandage does not squeeze the skin either; the wrap provides only “controlled compression”.

Lycon

Lycon is an Australian brand and a leader among depilatory waxes. It is valued for its high quality and aromatic scents based on natural aloe, chamomile, lavender, high-quality resin and beeswax. The specially developed formula nourishes and regenerates, leaving the skin soft and smooth. Lycon waxes are flexible and effective at removing even the shortest hairs (1 mm). The wide LYCON range meets every client’s expectations, with hard HOT WAX, soft STRIP WAX and Lycojet (ideal for sensitive areas such as Brazilian or facial waxing).
