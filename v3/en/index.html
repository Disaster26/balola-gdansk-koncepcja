Longevity: caring for yourself for the long run

At Balola, longevity is not just another treatment category - it is how we work. The aim is for your skin and body to stay in good condition not only after one visit, but for years to come.

  • RecoveryTime to rest, and treatments that support the natural renewal of your skin and body.
  • Mindful careTreatments matched to your skin and a home routine you can actually keep up.
  • Well-beingCalm, breathing and a moment for yourself matter as much as what you see in the mirror.

We combine relaxation and skilled hands with cosmetology and modern technologies. If longevity is new to you, start with a free 30-minute consultation.

Fifth floor, overlooking the Motława

Balola Gdańsk is located in Hotel Gdańsk Boutique at 9 Szafarnia Street. The treatment room windows look out over the Motława and the old granaries, and inside you will find a warm interior of beige and wood. The sea is close by, and the city rush stays outside.
